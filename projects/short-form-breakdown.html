Short Form Case Study

A deep dive into the cut: hooks, pacing, sound design, and polish.

Project Context

  • Client: Example Brand
  • Goal: Maximize retention on platform X
  • Role: Editor & Motion Designer
  • Result: +18% retention in first 7 days

Quick Notes

Rapid hook created from B-roll, layered sound design to sell motion, and aggressive audio ducking for clarity. Grade leaned cinematic with punchy mid-contrast. Visual effects focused on subtle compositing and cleanup.

Editing Strategy

The primary aim was an immediate hook in the first two seconds followed by rhythmically placed cuts synced to the main percussive hits. Sound design layering (foley, transient enhancement, and saturation) created perceived impact even at low volumes. Iterations focused on trimming to the most communicative action—if it didn't pull focus, it went.
